01 / ABOUT

Science driven by
mechanistic questions.

I am a Staff Scientist at Washington University School of Medicine in St. Louis, Missouri, with a research background in biochemistry, structural biology, biophysics, and host–virus interactions.

My research focuses on understanding how viral proteins interact with host factors and reprogram cellular processes. I am particularly interested in defining these interactions at molecular and structural resolution and connecting molecular mechanisms to biological function.

My doctoral research was conducted in the laboratory of Prof. Timir Tripathi at North-Eastern Hill University, India.

Current research direction

My current work investigates molecular mechanisms underlying respiratory syncytial virus (RSV) NS1 interactions with host transcriptional machinery, particularly the co-activator Mediator complex.

A major goal is to understand how viral proteins exploit host nuclear processes and to define these interactions using complementary structural, biochemical, biophysical, and cell-based approaches.
